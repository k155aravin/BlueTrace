# BlueTrace Support

For BlueTrace support, use:

support@aranmultiservices.com

## Helpful Information To Include

When reporting a problem, include:

- phone model
- Android version
- what you were trying to do
- what happened
- whether Bluetooth was on
- whether permissions were granted
- screenshots, if safe to share

Do not send private reports, screenshots, or device details if doing so would create a safety risk.

## Ideas And Feedback

BlueTrace is still evolving. Feedback and ideas are welcome at:

support@aranmultiservices.com

Possible future directions include scan history, safer export reports, stronger Bluetooth pattern analysis, and trusted contact safety check-ins.

Safety check-in ideas, such as sending a preset SMS or location share to a trusted person, must be designed carefully. They should always be user-controlled and should not be treated as a replacement for emergency services.

## Urgent Safety Issues

BlueTrace support is not an emergency service.

If you believe you are in immediate danger, leave the area and contact local emergency services.
