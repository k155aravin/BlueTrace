# BlueTrace User Guide

BlueTrace is an Android safety-awareness app that helps you notice recurring nearby Bluetooth devices across multiple locations.

BlueTrace does not identify people. It does not prove that someone is following you. It gives you a calm way to review Bluetooth patterns that may deserve attention.

## Basic Use

1. Open BlueTrace.
2. Choose a sweep style: Walking, Driving, Inside, or Manual.
3. Scan at your first location.
4. Move to a different location and scan again.
5. Move again and scan a third time.
6. Review the final result.

## Result Meanings

**All Clear**

No unknown device matched all three scan points.

**Watch**

An unknown device appeared at two scan points. This is a caution signal, not proof.

**Alert**

An unknown device appeared at all three scan points. Review the device details and consider running another sweep.

## Trusted Devices

Use Trusted Devices for your own devices, such as headphones, watches, laptops, car Bluetooth, or a second phone.

Trusted devices are ignored during security sweeps.

## Baseline Profile

Use the baseline profile in a quiet place, such as your home, car, or regular workspace.

The baseline teaches BlueTrace what is normal around you. Baseline matches are ignored during sweeps so normal devices do not keep creating concern.

## Share Report

After a completed sweep, BlueTrace can create a simple text report. The report includes:

- date and time
- scan locations
- Watch and Alert devices
- confidence score
- trusted and baseline counts
- safety disclaimer

Only share a report with people you trust.

## Important Safety Note

BlueTrace is an awareness tool. It is not legal proof, threat detection, identity confirmation, or a replacement for emergency services.

If you believe you are in immediate danger, leave the area and contact local emergency services.
