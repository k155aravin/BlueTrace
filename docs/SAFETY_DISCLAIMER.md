# BlueTrace Safety Disclaimer

BlueTrace is an experimental safety-awareness tool.

It helps users review recurring nearby Bluetooth signals across multiple locations.

BlueTrace does not guarantee:

- threat detection
- stalking detection
- identity confirmation
- exact distance measurement
- legal proof
- personal safety
- emergency response

Bluetooth signals can be hidden, randomized, duplicated, blocked, delayed, or misinterpreted.

BlueTrace results should be treated as awareness signals only. They should not replace judgment, emergency services, law enforcement, professional safety advice, or legal advice.

If you believe you are in immediate danger, leave the area and contact local emergency services.
