# BlueTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

BlueTrace is an Android safety-awareness app by Aran Multi Services Inc.

## Data BlueTrace Handles

BlueTrace may process nearby Bluetooth information, including:

- Bluetooth device address shown by Android
- device name, when available
- signal strength
- manufacturer information, when available
- scan timing information
- trusted device entries
- baseline profile entries

## Local Storage

The current BlueTrace prototype stores trusted devices and baseline profile data locally on the phone using Android local storage.

BlueTrace does not currently upload scan data to a cloud server.

## Permissions

BlueTrace requests Bluetooth and location-related permissions because Android requires them for Bluetooth Low Energy scanning.

BlueTrace uses these permissions to scan nearby Bluetooth signals during user-started scans.

## Sharing Reports

BlueTrace can create a text report after a sweep. Reports are shared only when the user chooses to share them through Android's share sheet.

Users should review reports before sharing.

## No Identity Guarantee

Bluetooth data does not reliably identify a person. BlueTrace does not claim to identify people, prove intent, or confirm that someone is following you.

## Children

BlueTrace is not designed for children. It is a safety-awareness prototype for general users.
